## Course Rules & Assessment
###  Grading Formula
The final grade is calculated based on two main components:
> **Final Grade** = **40%** (Milestones) + **60%** (Demo Day)

### How We Build
Every team builds **one AI-centred product** during the semester, adding one layer in each technical lab.
Each layer follows the same loop:

> **Specify** what it should do → **Build** it with AI → **Verify** that it does it

### Milestone Guidelines
* **Submission:** Each milestone is delivered in your **team's GitHub repository** (no MS Teams upload).
    * The repository is checked at the deadline.
* **Late Policy:**
    * ⚠️ **Penalty:** There is a **-2 points** penalty from the milestone grade for every **2 weeks of delay**.

### Participation Requirements
* **Labs:** Maximum **2 absences**.
* **Challenge Owner Meetings:** Teams are required to participate in meetings with the challenge owner to ensure project alignment.
* **Demo Day (Week 14):**
    * ❗ Participation is **mandatory** for **all** team members.

## Kiro for Students (1,000 free credits per month)
UBB is part of **Kiro for Students**: an AI coding assistant (IDE and agent) with **1,000 credits per month** for every student.

**Setup:**
1. Go to [aws.amazon.com/builder](https://aws.amazon.com/builder/) and create an **AWS Builder ID** with your **`<your_email>@stud.ubbcluj.ro`** address.
2. Open [kiro.dev/students](https://kiro.dev/students/).
3. Sign up to Kiro with the **AWS Builder ID** option. If you registered in step 1, it should log you in automatically.
4. Go to **Settings**: a pop-up should say that you are eligible for 1,000 credits.
5. Click **I'm a student** and follow the steps to register.

> Tip: start in Kiro's **spec mode** and give it your `SPECS.md`. Check the requirements, design and tasks it writes before you let it code.

## Teams & Classroom
Each team works in **one shared GitHub repository**, created through Classroom 50. All milestones are delivered there.

**Create your team repository:**
1. Open the assignment link: [**PPI team repository on Classroom 50**](https://classroom50.org/LauraDiosan-CS/mirpr-2026-2027/assignments/ppi/accept) and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. **One** team member creates the group: under **Group name**, type **`Echipa X`**, where **X** is your team number from the team sign-up spreadsheet (e.g. `Echipa 7`), then click **Create group**.
3. The other members open the same link, find **Echipa X** under **Join a group** and click **Ask to join**. The member who created the group approves the requests (or adds teammates directly via **Manage my group**).

> If the page says **Not a member yet**, accept the GitHub invitation to the organization (check your email), then click **Check again**.
