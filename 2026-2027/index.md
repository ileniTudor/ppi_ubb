## Course Rules & Assessment
###  Grading Formula
The final grade is calculated based on two main components:
> **Final Grade** = **40%** (Milestones) + **60%** (Demo Day)
---
### How We Build
Every team builds **one AI-centred product** during the semester, adding one layer in each technical lab.
Each layer follows the same loop:

> **Specify** what it should do → **Build** it with AI → **Verify** that it does it

Before you leave each lab, something new must be committed to your team repository.

### Milestones
There are **4 milestones**. Each one is due at the **start of your section's next technical lab**, so both sections get the same amount of time.

| Milestone | Due | What you deliver |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 – Spec** | Lab 2 | `SPEC.md`: target user and problem, innovation hypothesis, 3–5 acceptance criteria, initial AI approach, team roles. Write the first version yourselves, before asking an AI. |
| **M2 – Walking skeleton** | Lab 3 | The app runs and the core user flow works end to end, with the AI part mocked. A short self-review against the 10 usability heuristics. |
| **M3 – Real AI + evidence** | Lab 5 | Real AI integrated in the app (a generic or pre-trained model is fine; a fine-tuned model can replace it later behind the same interface). A golden set of test cases, an evaluation report compared to a baseline, and the reasoning behind your AI approach. |
| **M4 – Live & verified** | Lab 7 | A public URL, automated tests, validation of the AI output, handling of AI failures, and measured cost and latency. |

With every milestone, add a few lines to `JOURNAL.md`: what you decided, what the AI got wrong, and how you caught it.

### Milestone Guidelines
* **Submission:** Tag the milestone in your team repository (`m1`, `m2`, `m3`, `m4`) and upload the link on **MS Teams Assignments**.
    * *Note:* Only **one** team member needs to upload the link on behalf of the team.
* **Late Policy:**
    * ⚠️ **Penalty:** There is a **-2 points** penalty from the milestone grade for every **2 weeks of delay**.

### Participation Requirements
* **Labs:** Maximum **2 absences**.
* **Challenge Owner Meetings:** Teams are required to participate in meetings with the challenge owner to ensure project alignment.
* **Demo Day (Week 14):**
    * ❗ Participation is **mandatory** for **all** team members.
