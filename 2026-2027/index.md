## Course Rules & Assessment
###  Grading Formula
The final grade is calculated based on two main components:
> **Final Grade** = **40%** (Milestones) + **60%** (Demo Day)
---
### How We Build
Every team builds **one AI-centred product** during the semester, adding one layer in each technical lab.
Each layer follows the same loop:

> **Specify** what it should do → **Build** it with AI → **Verify** that it does it

### Milestone Guidelines
* **Submission:** Each milestone is delivered in your **team's GitHub repository** (no MS Teams upload).
    * The repository is checked at the deadline.
* **Late Policy:**
    * ⚠️ **Penalty:** There is a **-2 points** penalty from the milestone grade for every **2 weeks of delay**.

### Participation Requirements
* **Labs:** Maximum **2 absences**.
* **Challenge Owner Meetings:** Teams are required to participate in meetings with the challenge owner to ensure project alignment.
* **Demo Day (Week 14):**
    * ❗ Participation is **mandatory** for **all** team members.
